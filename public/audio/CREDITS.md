# Otterpuck audio

Music: **Electric Stream (180 BPM)** by **Alexander Ehlers / tricksntraps**, from [T & T Free Cyberpunk Pack 2](https://opengameart.org/content/t-t-free-cyberpunk-pack-2). Released by the author under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Original file: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/electric_stream_180bpm.ogg

Game edit: an 80-second loop, reduced peak level, softened boundary and stereo AAC encoding at approximately 192 kbps. Streamed from the game's own assets; no external service is needed during play.

Dive/surface recordings: **Peludo**, [Water Splash and sand footsteps](https://opengameart.org/content/water-splash-and-sand-footsteps), CC0. Derived from `splash1_0.wav` and `splash2_0.wav`. Leading silence removed, level shaped, short fades applied, resampled to mono 22.05 kHz PCM.

Puck contact, flick and goal effects are authored in `src/audio-effects.ts`. The old background oscillator melody and underwater drone are absent from this audio system.
