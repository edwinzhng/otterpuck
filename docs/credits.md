## Game art

The characters, equipment, arena geometry, vertex paint, and procedural surface treatments are authored for Otterpuck and covered by the project license. Character designs follow Edwin's supplied references. Alpine, forest, ruins, and desert scenery follows Edwin's concept sheet. Editable Blender sources are in `art/characters/` and `art/arenas/`; exported models are in `public/models/`.

The alpine, forest, ruins, desert, glacier, and terminal backdrops were generated for Otterpuck with OpenAI image generation. Their runtime files are `public/art/arenas/<arena>-panorama-painted.webp`. These painted mountain, forest, desert, arctic, and harbor panoramas contain no third-party images and are covered by the project license.
